Handling Special Dietary Requests

1. Words & Phrases

Complimentary Adjective

Meaning: Provided for free as a courtesy or service.

"We offer complimentary breakfast for all guests in the main dining hall."

2. The Article

Paragraph 1: In modern hospitality, accommodating guest food allergies and dietary restrictions is critical. Front-of-house staff must accurately communicate ingredient details between the guest and the kitchen to avoid health risks.

Paragraph 2: When a request cannot be met immediately, offering a complimentary alternative demonstrates high-quality service and preserves customer loyalty.
